# Cloudflare Business SLA (public terms, archived 21 July 2019)

Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20190721090147/https://www.cloudflare.com/business-sla/

Lines starting with ">" are quoted from that page.

> 1.1 100% Uptime. The Service will serve Customer Content 100% of the time without qualification.
> 1.2 Penalties. If the Service fails to meet the above service level, the Customer will receive a credit equal to the result of the Service Credit calculation in Section 6 of this SLA.
> 3.2 ... the Customer must first have notified Customer Support of the Incident ... within five business days following the Incident.
> 5.2 Service Credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any violation of this SLA.
> 5.3 The total amount of Service Credits awarded in any twelve (12) month period shall not, under any circumstance, exceed one (1) month of a Customer's cumulative total monthly service fees.
> 6.1 ... Service Credit = (Outage Period minutes * Affected Customer Ratio) ÷ Scheduled Availability minutes

## Worked out by Ripple, not stated by Cloudflare
A 27 minute outage in July (44,640 minutes) that reaches every visitor is a credit of at most 0.06% of the month's fee.
The Business plan list price in 2019 was $200 a month.
