# Postmortem INC-088: ledger-db ran out of connections

Started: 2026-06-10T08:55:00Z
Resolved: 2026-06-10T10:10:00Z

## Summary
ledger-db refused new connections for 75 minutes. Payouts and charges failed.

## Root cause
PR-301 scaled payouts-service to 12 replicas without lowering its connection pool.

## Resolution
Replicas were scaled back to 4. A per-service connection cap was proposed.
