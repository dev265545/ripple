# Postmortem INC-101: Payments timeouts from a ledger retry storm

Started: 2026-08-02T07:00:00Z
Resolved: 2026-08-02T10:05:00Z

## Summary
Charges timed out for three hours. checkout-api returned 5xx to merchants.

## Root cause
PR-377 retried failed ledger writes immediately and without a limit, multiplying load on ledger-db.

## Resolution
PR-380 added exponential backoff and a retry limit.
